How it works

Three steps. No app for shoppers.

ScanExit runs in the shopper's phone browser. They start from a QR banner at your entrance and finish at the exit check system.

1

Scan the banner

Shoppers scan the QR at your entrance and enter their phone number. They get a word that only they can use to claim their order.

2

Scan and pay as they shop

Each barcode they scan drops into the basket at your price. When they're done, they pay on the phone by card or transfer.

3

The exit check, then out

Their phone shows a one-time ScanExit pass. At the exit they scan it at the exit check system and place their items on it; the AI camera checks every item.

The exit check system

Every item checked. Automatically.

A smart conveyor at your exit with an AI camera. It checks each item the shopper unloads against what they paid for, so your staff only step in when something doesn't match.

  • Scan the pass at the exit checkThe shopper's paid list appears on the exit check screen.
  • Place items on the conveyorItems pass the AI camera one at a time and tick off the list.
  • Green: bag and goEverything matches, the pass is used up, and the shopper leaves.
  • Red: your attendant fixes only that itemThe screen shows exactly what's missing or extra, and what to collect or refund.
What your store gets

Meet the ScanExit exit check system.

A compact smart conveyor that sits at your exit. Installed and set up by us, included in your plan.

  1. AI cameraLooks down on the conveyor, reads each item's barcode and recognises the product.
  2. Entry sensorCounts every item as it goes onto the conveyor, so nothing slips past uncounted.
  3. Conveyor motorMoves items under the camera one at a time, at a steady speed.
  4. Exit sensorConfirms each item has passed the camera before it reaches the bagging end.
  5. Status lightGreen when the basket matches the receipt; red calls your attendant.
  6. Vision AI computerBuilt into the frame. It matches what the camera sees against the shopper's paid receipt and shows the result on the exit check screen.

Questions

What store owners ask us.

Do shoppers need to download an app?

No. They scan the QR banner at your entrance and ScanExit opens in their phone's browser.

What if a customer doesn't scan something?

Every item goes through the exit check system, and its AI camera checks each one against the paid pass. An unpaid item turns the screen red, and your attendant collects for it there and then.

What if the exit check system is down?

Your staff switch to the manual check: they scan the shopper's pass on a phone or tablet and check the basket by hand until the exit check system is back.

What about customers who don't want to use it?

Nothing changes for them. Your normal tills stay open; ScanExit simply takes the pressure off them at peak times.

Does it work with our POS?

We start from a product export from your POS (barcodes, names and prices) and keep it updated. A live sync with your POS can follow.

How do payments work?

Shoppers pay by card or transfer through Paystack. An order only counts as paid once Paystack confirms it, so fake transfer alerts don't work.
